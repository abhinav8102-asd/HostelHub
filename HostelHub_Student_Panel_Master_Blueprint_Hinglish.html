🎯 Document Overview: Ye document HostelHub ke Student Portal (frontend-user & Mobile App) ke har ek function, feature, authentication security, real-time chat, complaint engine, mess ratings, aur mobile/desktop UI layout ka 100% complete master breakdown provide karta hai.

1 🔑 Student Authentication & Security Engine
🔐 Account Access & Onboarding Features Auth Engine
  • Email & Password Login: Secure login with JWT Token storage in browser/app state for seamless sessions.
  • Google OAuth 2.0 Single Sign-On: 1-Click "Sign in with Google" integration for instant login without typing passwords.
  • Student Registration Signup: New student onboarding form requiring Full Name, Email, Password, Phone Number, Gender (Male/Female), and Batch selection. New accounts get pending_verification status until Admin/Warden approval.
  • Forgot Password & Email OTP Verification: 6-Digit OTP sent to registered email for password recovery with countdown expiration timer.
  • In-App Change Password: Secure password change from Profile settings page.
2 👤 Student Profile & Account Details
🪪 Digital Student ID & Personal Details Profile Management
  • Profile Overview Card: View Name, Roll Number, Allocated Room Number, Hostel Block, Roll No, Batch (e.g. Batch 2025), Gender, Phone, and Bio.
  • Profile Picture Upload: Upload and change profile avatar directly from mobile camera or gallery (Powered by backend Multer storage engine).
  • Editable Student Bio & Contact: Personal bio and contact phone number updates.
3 🛠️ Complaint Lodging & Real-Time Tracking Engine
📝 Complaint Registration & Media Attachment Core Feature
  • Multi-Category Complaint Registration: Raise maintenance or misconduct issues under categories:
    • 🚰 Plumbing: Water leakage, tap repair, flush issue.
    • ⚡ Electrical: Fan, light bulb, switchboard, AC fault.
    • 📡 Wi-Fi / IT: Network downtime, router issue, slow speed.
    • 🍽️ Mess & Food: Food quality, hygiene, mess hall issues.
    • 🪑 Furniture / Carpentry: Door lock, bed, study table repair.
    • ⚖️ Misconduct / Disciplinary: Noise disturbance, rule violations.
  • Camera Photo Evidence Upload: Take photo directly from mobile camera or upload from gallery as proof.
  • Real-Time Status Timeline Badges:
    • 🟡 Pending: Complaint raised, waiting for warden review.
    • 🔵 In Progress: Warden assigned maintenance staff (e.g. Electrician Ramesh working).
    • 🟢 Resolved: Work finished & verified.
    • 🔴 Rejected: Invalid or duplicate request with warden reason.
  • Direct Complaint Chat Thread: Chat and add comments on specific complaints directly with the Warden or assigned Technician.
4 📅 Attendance Tracker & Monthly Calendar
📊 Student Attendance Dashboard Attendance Engine
  • Real-Time Today's Attendance Status: Instant status check whether marked 🟢 Present, 🔴 Absent, or 🟡 Outstation Leave for the day.
  • Monthly Attendance Calendar Grid: Interactive calendar showing daily attendance history with color-coded day badges.
  • Attendance Percentage Gauge: Visual percentage ring (e.g., 94% Attendance) to track semester attendance requirement.
5 🍽️ Mess Operations, Daily Menu & 5-Star Feedback
🍱 Daily Mess Menu & Food Quality Rating Mess Module
  • Daily & Weekly Mess Menu: View today's and full week's meal schedule:
    • 🍳 Breakfast: 7:30 AM - 9:00 AM
    • 🍲 Lunch: 12:30 PM - 2:00 PM
    • ☕ Evening Snacks: 5:00 PM - 6:00 PM
    • 🍛 Dinner: 8:00 PM - 9:30 PM
  • 1-to-5 Star Meal Feedback Submission: Submit star ratings and food quality comments for any meal.
  • Skip Meal Requests: Mark meal skip requests before cutoff time for food wastage control.
6 💬 Real-Time Chat & Community Messaging
⚡ Socket.io Instant Messaging System Real-Time Chat
  • 1-on-1 Direct Messaging: Direct private chat channel with Hostel Warden and other Students.
  • Hostel Block & Floor Group Chat Rooms: Group chat channels for all residents of specific hostel blocks (e.g., *Boys Block A Main Chat*).
  • Real-Time Socket.io Delivery: Instant message delivery without page refresh.
  • Unread Message Counter Badges: Dynamic red unread badge counters on chat tabs.
  • Media & Attachment Sharing: Share photos and documents inside chat.
7 📢 Announcements, Circulars & Push Alerts
🔔 Live Notification & Notice Board Alerts
  • Official Hostel Notice Board: View official circulars and notices published by Warden and Management.
  • Real-Time Push Notifications: Instant alert popups for:
    • Complaint Status Updates (e.g. "Your plumbing complaint #104 has been marked Resolved")
    • New Official Announcements
    • Warden Direct Messages
