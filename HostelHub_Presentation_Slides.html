PROJECT VISION & OBJECTIVES

Problem Statement & HostelHub Vision

SLIDE 02 / 10
⚠️ Traditional Hostel Pain Points
  • Manual Register Attendance: High proxy error rates and physical record loss.
  • Unorganized Repair Dispatch: Long delays in electrician and plumber assignment.
  • Food Waste in Mess: No prior tracking of meal skips causing excess cooking.
  • Unverified Communication: Fragmented chat groups with zero warden moderation.
  • No Proof Accountability: Service staff resolving tickets without proof photo upload.
🎯 The HostelHub Solution
  • 100% Digital Workflow: Unifies Students, Wardens, Staff & Admins in real-time.
  • Automated Ticket Dispatching: Category-based routing with resolution SLA timeline.
  • Mandatory Resolution Proof: Staff uploads job completion photo before closing ticket.
  • Hostel Block Roll Call: Instant filterable statistics (Total, Present, Absent).
  • Evening Snacks & Skip System: Cuts mess food waste by up to 30%.

WORKFLOW PIPELINE

4-Stage Ticket Resolution Lifecycle

SLIDE 05 / 10
1. Raise Ticket

Student submits issue title, description, category, priority tag & issue photo attachment.

2. Assign Staff

Warden reviews new complaints & assigns available electrician, plumber or technician.

3. Work Progress

Staff marks job 'In Progress', inspects site & conducts physical maintenance repair.

4. Proof & Feedback

Staff uploads completion photo proof. Student inspects work & rates 1-5 stars.

ENGINEERING & SECURITY

Tech Stack & Critical Security Guards

SLIDE 09 / 10
⚙️ Full Stack Technology
  • Frontend: Angular v19 Standalone, TypeScript, RxJS, Vanilla CSS.
  • Mobile APK: Capacitor Native Android Bridge & Back-Button Guard.
  • Backend API: Node.js, Express.js REST Controllers & Auth Middlewares.
  • Database: PostgreSQL with Sequelize ORM.
  • Cloud Storage: Supabase Storage with Base64 fallback.
🔒 Security Enforcements
  • Profile Re-Approval Rule: Editing Gender or Batch triggers critical warning & requires Warden Re-approval.
  • Dual-Press Back Exit Guard: Back button navigates to Home tab first; second press opens Exit Confirmation Modal.
  • JWT Role Protection: Route guards for Student, Warden, Staff & Admin.
